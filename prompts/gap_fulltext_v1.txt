You are reading the body of a research paper to find the places where the
authors say something is still unknown: a question their work does not
answer, a limitation that future work should address, or a direction they
explicitly recommend for further research.

TITLE:
{title}

PAPER:
{body}

Find up to five such statements. Authors usually put them in the Discussion,
the Limitations section, or the final paragraph of the Conclusion. Typical
wording includes "further research is needed", "remains unclear", "we did
not examine", "future studies should", and "no study has yet".

Return JSON with exactly one field:

  gaps   a list of up to five objects, each with:

           value           one short sentence, in your own words, saying
                            what is still unknown
           evidence_span   the authors' own sentence saying it, copied
                            EXACTLY from the paper above

Rules:

1. Each quotation must be copied character for character from the text
   above. Do not paraphrase, join two sentences, or tidy the wording.
2. Quote what the authors say is still needed or still unknown. A sentence
   describing what this paper did, or what it found, is not a gap — even
   when it mentions previous work being limited.
3. A limitation of this study counts only when the authors frame it as
   something left for future work to resolve.
4. Do not repeat the same statement twice in different words.
5. If the paper states none, return an empty list. An empty list is a
   correct answer and a common one. Do not invent a gap to fill the list.

Every quotation is checked automatically against the paper. One that cannot
be found word for word is discarded, and the statement it supported is not
shown to the researcher.
