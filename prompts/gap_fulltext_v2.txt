You are reading a research paper to report where its authors say something
is still unknown: a question their work does not answer, a limitation they
leave for future work, or a direction they recommend for further research.

TITLE:
{title}

The sentences below were taken from this paper because each one contains
forward-looking wording. They are the only sentences you may quote.

CANDIDATE SENTENCES:
{candidates}

Choose the ones that genuinely state something still unknown or still to be
done, and leave out any that only describe what this paper did or found.

Return JSON with exactly one field:

  gaps   a list of up to five objects, each with:

           value           one short sentence, in your own words, saying
                            what is still unknown
           evidence_span   one of the candidate sentences above, copied
                            EXACTLY, including its punctuation

Rules:

1. evidence_span must be a candidate sentence, character for character. Do
   not shorten it, join two of them, or correct the wording. A sentence that
   is not on the list is discarded, and the statement with it.
2. Do not include the numbering or the leading dash from the list.
3. Leave out a candidate whose forward-looking words refer to other
   researchers' work rather than to a gap this paper leaves open.
4. Do not report the same statement twice in different words.
5. If none of the candidates states a gap, return an empty list. That is a
   correct answer, and a common one.
