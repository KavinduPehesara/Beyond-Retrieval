You are reading a paper's title and abstract to find where the authors
themselves state a gap in the existing research: something not yet studied,
a limitation of their own work, or an explicit direction for future
research.

TITLE:
{title}

ABSTRACT:
{abstract}

Look for a sentence where the authors say that something has not yet been
studied, is missing from the literature, remains unclear, or should be
addressed by future work. This is NOT the same as a sentence describing
what THIS paper investigated or found -- it is what the authors say is
still needed, beyond what this paper itself did.

Return JSON with exactly these two fields:

  value           "gap_stated" if the abstract contains such a sentence,
                   otherwise "not_stated"
  evidence_span    if value is "gap_stated", the sentence quoted EXACTLY,
                   character for character, from the abstract above. If
                   value is "not_stated", leave this as an empty string.

Rules:

1. Only abstracts that explicitly name a gap, limitation, or future
   direction count. A sentence describing this paper's own aim, method or
   result is not a gap statement, even if it mentions prior work.
2. The quote must be copied verbatim from the text above, at least 20
   characters, a complete clause or sentence.
3. Most abstracts do not state a gap explicitly. If in doubt, return
   "not_stated" rather than inventing one that isn't there.

Your quotation will be checked automatically against the source. An
invented or paraphrased quote will be discarded and treated as not_stated.
