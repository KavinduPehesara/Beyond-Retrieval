You are extracting structured information from the full text of a research
paper for a systematic review.

Extract exactly five fields. For each one, give the value and the single
sentence from the paper that states it.

FIELDS

primary_outcome
    The main result the paper reports, with its number if one is given.
    What did they find, quantitatively?

effect_size
    The magnitude of the main effect and its uncertainty: an odds ratio,
    risk ratio, hazard ratio, correlation coefficient, mean difference,
    regression coefficient, or similar, together with its confidence
    interval or p-value if the paper gives one.

statistical_methods
    The analysis used to produce that result: the test, model or procedure
    named by the authors.

sample_characteristics
    Who or what was studied, and how many: the number of participants,
    datasets, projects or records, and anything the authors say about who
    they were.

limitations
    A weakness, constraint or unanswered question the authors state about
    their own work. Only what they say themselves. Do not infer one.

RULES FOR THE EVIDENCE SPAN

1. The span must be copied WORD FOR WORD from the paper text below. It is
   checked automatically against that text. A single changed, added or
   removed word makes the answer fail, and a failed answer is discarded.

2. Copy from the PAPER TEXT only. Do not copy any part of these
   instructions, these field descriptions, or the field names. Those are
   not evidence.

3. Quote one complete sentence where you can. It must be long enough to
   stand on its own as evidence — at least twenty characters.

4. Do not join two sentences from different parts of the paper into one
   span. If the evidence is split, quote the single sentence that states
   the field most directly.

5. Do not reword, summarise, tidy up, correct, translate or shorten the
   sentence. Copy it exactly as printed, including its numbers and
   punctuation.

6. If the paper does not state a field, set its value to exactly
   "not_stated" and its evidence_span to "". Do not guess, and do not
   infer a value from related information. "not_stated" is a correct and
   useful answer.

OUTPUT

Return JSON only, with this exact shape and nothing else:

{{
  "primary_outcome":        {{"value": "...", "evidence_span": "..."}},
  "effect_size":            {{"value": "...", "evidence_span": "..."}},
  "statistical_methods":    {{"value": "...", "evidence_span": "..."}},
  "sample_characteristics": {{"value": "...", "evidence_span": "..."}},
  "limitations":            {{"value": "...", "evidence_span": "..."}}
}}

{title}
